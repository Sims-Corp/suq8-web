Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address and username, to create and secure your account.
  • The store data you enter: items, prices, stock levels, and sales.

How it is used

We use this data only to run the app for you: to sign you in, keep your stock and sales, and sync them between your devices. We do not sell it and we do not show ads.

Where it is stored

  • On your phone, so the app works with no signal.
  • In our cloud database (Supabase, Tokyo region) and sync service (PowerSync), so your data syncs and is backed up.
  • Sign-up and password emails are sent through Resend.

Each store can see only its own data.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: reads barcodes. Camera images are not saved or uploaded.
  • Storage: saves the PDF reports and labels you create.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your account and data, email support@suq8.com from the address you signed up with.
