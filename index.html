SUQ8

A point of sale that keeps working when the signal drops

SUQ8 helps a suki tindahan track stock and ring up sales from an Android phone. Every sale is saved on the phone first and syncs to the cloud when there is signal.

Early access

SUQ8 is in a small pilot with a few stores. To ask for an install link, email hello@suq8.com.

What it does

Know your stock

Every item with its brand, unit and barcode, and a low-stock view.

Sell in a few taps

Ring up a sale quickly, with the day's figures on one screen.

Works offline

Sales are saved on the phone first, then catch up by themselves.

English and Filipino

Built for the way Filipino stores talk about sales and profit.
